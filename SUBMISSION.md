## Project introduction
EDUAI helps prospective undergraduate students explore universities and turn study goals into a clear preparation plan. Users can search university profiles, filter destinations, save up to three options for comparison, and create a pathway based on their study interest, education stage, destination and intended start month. A checklist tracks progress and can be exported. The interface works across desktop and mobile layouts, and saved choices remain in the same browser.

## How the rules support future development
The project separates university content from the interface. AGENTS.md defines the required university fields, official-source requirements, shared visual style, file structure, storage behavior and verification steps. Adding a university record automatically makes it available in discovery, its profile and the comparison table. A new destination requires matching filter and planner options. These conventions let future pages and content follow a consistent structure without rebuilding the site. Planning dates remain explicitly separate from official application deadlines.
